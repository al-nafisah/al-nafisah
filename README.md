## Hi, I'm Abdullah Al-Nafisah

I'm an R&D engineer. I build FPGA, embedded and RF systems for quantum hardware and communications. I hold an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KAUST and a BSc in Electrical Engineering and Physics (double major, First Honors) from KFUPM.

### Active projects

- **Edge LLM Inference on FPGAs.** A pre-trained language model split across four AMD Kria KV260 boards that decode one token stream together and serve chat locally, with no cloud and no GPU. [Project page](https://al-nafisah.com/projects/llm-kv260)
- **Real-Time Image Classification on FPGA.** A binarized CNN in the fabric of a PYNQ-Z2 labels 1280x720 HDMI video at 31.1 fps, with the whole chip drawing 2.65 W. [Code](https://github.com/al-nafisah/fpga-video-classifier)
- **Turbo-Equalized FTN-Inspired Modulations** (MSc thesis, KAUST). With turbo equalization and convolutional and LDPC decoding, they gain 2.3 dB over turbo-equalized 4-ASK at a BER of 10⁻⁵, at the same two bits per channel use. [Thesis](https://doi.org/10.25781/KAUST-M5140) · [Code](https://github.com/al-nafisah/coded-msprs)


<details>
<summary>Archived projects</summary>

- **Magnetic Field Gradiometer.** Four magnetometers on the faces of a 3 cm cube, read by an ESP32. Differencing opposite faces gives two columns of the magnetic field gradient tensor. [Code](https://github.com/al-nafisah/cube-gradiometer-esp32)
- **Acoustic Ranging on FPGA.** Three Artix-7 boards time a Zadoff-Chu coded sound burst and find its arrival by FFT cross-correlation in hardware. Over the air, from 0.25 to 1 m, mean error was 1.3 to 3.5 cm after outlier rejection. It was the real-time hardware for R. Al Nafisi's MSc thesis at KAUST. [Code](https://github.com/al-nafisah/fpga-acoustic-localization)
- **Wireless Seismic Data Acquisition.** A cable-free seismic recorder, our five-person KFUPM senior design project. In a field test it recorded the same first arrival as the Geophysics Department's cabled recorder. [Code](https://github.com/al-nafisah/wireless-seismic-daq)
